Our Culinary Journey

A Decade of Authentic Flavors & Passion

Founded in 2014, Maison du Curry brings traditional Indian & Sri-Lankan recipes directly to 51 Rue du Taur and Marché Victor Hugo in Toulouse. We select the finest spices, slow-simmer our gravies, and serve fresh thalis, samosas, and naan daily.

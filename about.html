Spécialités Indiennes et Sri-Lankaises

The Story of Maison du Curry

From a passion for authentic spice blends to a landmark culinary brand in Toulouse.

Passion & Integrity

Authentic Indian & Sri-Lankan Gastronomy

At Maison du Curry, food is a celebration of heritage, family traditions, and warm hospitality. Every gravy is slow-cooked from scratch without artificial additives or thickeners, using hand-selected cardamom, cloves, turmeric, and Kashmiri chillies.

Our team delivers daily traiteur catering services, fresh thali platters, samosas, and roasted tandoori delicacies across Toulouse.

Milestones

Our Growth Timeline

Key chapters in our decade-long journey across Toulouse.

2014

Foundation of Maison du Curry

Launched in Toulouse with a vision to offer authentic, high-quality Indian & Sri-Lankan gastronomy.

Marché Victor Hugo

Covered Market Traiteur Counter

Opened our landmark traiteur stall inside Toulouse's historic covered market Place Victor Hugo.

Halles de la Cartoucherie

Street Food Expansion

Expanded into Les Halles de la Cartoucherie, serving fast gourmet Indian street food and fresh naan.

Present & Future

Corporate Dining & Airbus Branch

Established on-site corporate dining service at Airbus campus and expanded restaurant operations.
